Projects

Saluyot biocomposite tomato coating

Senior high research testing a saluyot (Corchorus olitorius) mucilage–gelatin film as an edible postharvest coating. Completely randomized design, analyzed with Welch’s one-way ANOVA (p = 0.168, not significant), and defended orally.

  • Experimental design
  • Welch’s ANOVA
  • Research writing

Piggy Bank simulator

A Python bank transaction simulator. It handles single transactions with if/elif/else and match-case, then loops until the user exits and rejects invalid menu choices.

  • Python
  • Control flow
  • Input validation
