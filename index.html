About me

Hello, I’m Chezka

I’m Chezka Marie Vendiola, a first-year BS Data Science student at the University of the Cordilleras in Baguio City. I’m curious about how things work, and data is how I look for answers.

My first taste of research came in senior high, where I tested a saluyot-based coating to keep tomatoes fresh longer. It taught me to design an experiment, analyze the results honestly, and defend my choices.

Right now I’m building my foundation in Python, statistics, and SQL, with the goal of becoming a data scientist. When I’m not studying, I enjoy board games, movies, and travelling.
